# SkillPathai# 🏙️ District Intelligence Dashboard

> **One Platform for Real-Time District Intelligence, Citizen Awareness & Governance Transparency**

District Intelligence Dashboard is a civic-tech platform designed to bring important district-level information into one unified, easy-to-understand interface.

The current prototype focuses on **Krishna and Guntur districts of Andhra Pradesh**, with an architecture designed to scale from:

**District → State → India**

---

## 🚀 What is the Project?

District-level information is often distributed across different government portals, public datasets, APIs, reports, and other sources.

The District Intelligence Dashboard brings this information together and presents it through a single platform covering:

- Governance
- Government projects
- Government schemes
- Infrastructure
- Water
- Power
- Environment
- Public safety
- Healthcare
- Agriculture
- Economy
- Citizen services
- RTI
- Public grievances
- News and alerts

The goal is to turn fragmented public information into **accessible district intelligence**.

---

# 🎯 Problem Statement

Citizens often need to visit multiple websites and platforms to find information about their district.

Information related to:

- Government projects
- Scheme implementation
- Budget and spending
- Water supply
- Electricity
- Roads
- Healthcare
- Police and safety
- Agriculture
- Environment
- RTI and grievances
- Public services

may be distributed across different sources and presented in formats that are difficult to understand quickly.

This creates an information gap between available public data and citizens who need it.

---

# 💡 Our Solution

The District Intelligence Dashboard provides a unified interface where citizens can explore important district-level information and monitor documented progress of public projects and services.

The platform combines:

**Public APIs + Government/Public Sources + Datasets + Data Processing + Analytics**

into a single dashboard.
